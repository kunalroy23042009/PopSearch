Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about PopSearch.

How does PopSearch analyze channels?

We use the YouTube Data API to fetch channel statistics, recent videos, and engagement metrics. Our AI then analyzes this data to generate niche profiles, growth predictions, and content recommendations.

Is there a free plan?

Yes! Our free plan includes 3 channel analyses per month, basic search across YouTube and Reddit, and competitor discovery. No credit card required.

Which platforms are supported for content discovery?

We search across YouTube, Reddit, X (Twitter), TikTok, Twitch, Instagram, Hacker News, RSS feeds, and Google Trends — 15+ sources in a single query.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time through the billing portal. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.
